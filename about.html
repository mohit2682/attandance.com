Kept like a ledger, not a database

Every clock-in, entered like a line in the book.

Time Ledger is a punch-in attendance and payroll tracker for small teams — the kind of place that used to keep a paper register on the counter. It still feels like one. It just does the arithmetic for you.

How the math gets done

1
Set the shift
Enter each employee's monthly salary. Everyone follows the office's default hours unless they're given a shift of their own.
2
Punch in, punch out
Late minutes, early leaves, absences, and overtime are all timed to the minute against that shift — not guessed at the end of the month.
3
Export the month
Pull a spreadsheet with each day's pay already reduced for lateness and topped up for overtime, ready to hand to payroll.
Kept on your device. Time Ledger has no server and no sign-up — attendance and pay records are stored in this browser only. Use Settings → Export Backup to keep a copy safe, and to move records to another device.
